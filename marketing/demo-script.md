# 20-second demo script (Shorts / TikTok / Reels / YouTube)

Short vertical video is the highest-reach free channel for this. Keep it under 25s, no talking
head required, captions on screen.

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | On-screen caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Phone in hand shows YouTube playing a video | "Want YouTube on Android Auto?" |
| 0:03–0:06 | Plug phone in / Android Auto connects | "It's blocked by default…" |
| 0:06–0:10 | Quick shot of MyCar starting the mirror (consent prompt flashes) | "…until you mirror the screen" |
| 0:10–0:16 | Car screen: YouTube playing full-bleed; a small Maps "still navigating" callout | "Any app. And Maps keeps navigating" |
| 0:16–0:20 | Car screen with the quick-launch strip visible, tap YouTube | "Free · open source · no root" |
| 0:20–0:22 | End card: guide URL | "mstipanov.github.io/MyCar" |

## Caption / description

> Play YouTube (or any app) on your Android Auto screen — without losing Google Maps.
> MyCar is a free, open-source screen mirror. Sideload-only, no root.
> Use as a passenger or while parked. Not affiliated with Google/YouTube.
> Full guide: https://mstipanov.github.io/MyCar/ #AndroidAuto #CarTech #YouTube

## What to actually film

- A clean car (or the Desktop Head Unit emulator if you don't want to film in the car).
- Real YouTube playback, not a mockup.
- If you show Maps alongside, make it obvious it's still navigating — that's the whole hook.
- Vertical 9:16. Burn the captions in (most people watch muted).
- Post the same clip to Shorts, TikTok and Reels; pin a comment with the guide link.

## Do not

- Don't film while driving. Parked, or passenger filming.
- Don't imply Google/YouTube endorse it.
