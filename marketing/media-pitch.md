# Media &amp; blogger pitch

Goal: get MyCar into an existing "best sideloaded Android Auto apps" roundup, or trigger a short
write-up. These outlets already cover this exact niche, so the pitch is a natural fit — no money
involved.

## Who to pitch (tip lines / editors)

| Outlet | Where | Angle to lead with |
|---|---|---|
| XDA Developers | tip@xda-developers.com / their contact form | "Sideloaded AA screen mirror that keeps the nav slot" |
| Android Authority | their tip/contact form | "The one thing AA video apps get wrong: they kill Maps" |
| Android Police | tips@androidpolice.com | "Open-source AA mirror, no Play Store needed" |
| Engadget | tips@engadget.com | "You can mirror your phone to Android Auto without root" |
| aahacks / cartechstudio blogs | contact page | Request inclusion in their "best apps" lists |

Also worth a short post on the **XDA forums** Android Auto thread and any "AAAD / AAStore
alternatives" community, since that's where the audience already is.

## Pitch email (short — keep it under ~200 words)

**Subject:** Sideloaded Android Auto mirror that keeps Google Maps in the nav slot (open source)

> Hi &lt;editor&gt;,
>
> I read your piece on sideloaded Android Auto apps — I think there's a gap worth covering.
>
> CarStream, Fermata Auto and Screen2Auto all take over the navigation slot, so you lose Google
> Maps the moment you play anything. I built **MyCar**, a free, open-source (MIT) Android Auto
> screen mirror that can register as a *weather* app instead, so Maps/Waze keep routing while the
> mirror is on the car screen. It mirrors any app (YouTube, Netflix, a browser) and forwards touch
> back to the phone. No root.
>
> It's sideload-only (KingInstaller + *Unknown sources*), and I'm upfront that it can't show
> `FLAG_SECURE` content. Step-by-step guide and APK: https://mstipanov.github.io/MyCar/
> Source: https://github.com/mstipanov/MyCar
>
> Happy to provide screenshots, a demo video, or answer anything. Totally understand if it's not a
> fit.
>
> Thanks,
> Marko Stipanov

## Assets to attach or link

- Screenshots: phone + car screen with YouTube playing, and Maps visible alongside.
- A 20-second demo clip (see `demo-script.md`).
- The guide URL and the GitHub release URL.
- One-line description and the license.

## Notes

- Send individually; never BCC a blast.
- One follow-up after ~1 week, then stop.
- Pitch the *angle* (keeps the nav slot), not the app.
- Never claim it's affiliated with Google, and don't imply Google endorses it.
